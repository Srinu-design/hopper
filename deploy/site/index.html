Project 1

Hopper: a job queue
on PostgreSQL.

At-least-once delivery

A killed worker's jobs come back when its lease runs out, and finish on another.

Retries with backoff

Full-jitter exponential backoff, with limits set per task.

Dead-letter queue and replay

Jobs that keep failing are parked with their history, and can be replayed.

Multi-tenant with rate limits

Each tenant sees only its own jobs and has its own rate limit and queue quota.

Project 2

Sift: answers that
cite their sources.

Cites every claim

Every sentence cites a numbered source, and code rejects any citation that does not point to a retrieved chunk.

Refuses without evidence

A low rerank score refuses before any model call, and the model itself may refuse.

Every retrieval change measured

The retrieval eval runs on every pull request and fails the build on a regression.

Each customer sees only its own documents

Tenant filters plus row-level security in Postgres, tested for chunks, answers and cache entries.
